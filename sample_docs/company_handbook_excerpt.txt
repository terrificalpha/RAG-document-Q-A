Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Core collaboration hours are 10 AM to 3 PM in the employee's local time zone.
Remote employees must attend the quarterly in-person planning week.

Expense Reimbursement

Home office equipment up to $500 per year is reimbursable with a receipt.
Internet reimbursement is capped at $40 per month.
Reimbursement requests must be submitted within 60 days of purchase.
